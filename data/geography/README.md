# Missouri location coordinates

CareConnect uses the U.S. Census Bureau's 2025 Gazetteer files to resolve
Missouri city, town, village, Census-designated place, and county names to
representative latitude and longitude coordinates.

Included source files:

- `2025_gaz_place_29.txt`
- `2025_gaz_counties_29.txt`

Source: https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.2025.html

The coordinates are representative internal points for geographic areas. They
support approximate distance ranking; they are not a patient's precise
location and must not be treated as driving-distance calculations.
